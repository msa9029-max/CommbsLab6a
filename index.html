Panel 1

The donut is sitting in confectionery class surrounded by donuts with holes. She notices that she is different.

Panel 2

She looks at all the other donuts showing off their holes and decides that she needs one too.

Panel 3

She finds a tool and decides to make a hole herself. She tests it out on a piece of dough.

"How hard can it be?"

Panel 4

She makes the hole and all of her jam starts exploding and bleeding nonstop.

Panel 5

She is lying flat on the table with jelly everywhere and slowly losing herself and becoming deformed. Another donut asks if she is okay.

Panel 6

The donut is dead and lying alone on the table. After all her jelly has leaked out, she got her hole but at what cost?
